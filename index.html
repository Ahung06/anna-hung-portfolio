Unified Engineering Flight Competition

May 2026

Designed and built a competition RC aircraft optimized for complex scoring criteria. The aircraft was evaluated on speed and payload positioning, requiring balance between aerodynamic performance and structural design. Our team achieved first place in the speed flight and second overall.

Design Approach & Achievements:

  • Developed custom optimizer for aspect ratio, wing area, and velocity
  • Validated design using vortex lattice analysis
  • Analyzed spiral stability and selected 8° dihedral angle for recovery
  • Prioritized robust, predictable design over aggressive optimization
  • First place in speed flight; Second place overall
